My Story

Hi! I'm Justine May Balbuena, an Information Systems graduate specializing in Business Analytics from West Visayas State University.

I have professional experience in administrative support, sales operations, and virtual assistance, where I've developed skills in managing records, handling business correspondence, preparing reports, organizing data, and supporting daily business operations.

My background also includes internship experience in a healthcare setting, where I worked with administrative and accounting records and developed an Excel-based forecasting model and dashboard for patient visits.

Beyond administrative work, I'm passionate about using technology to make everyday processes more efficient. I enjoy working with data, building web solutions, exploring digital automation, and finding practical ways to simplify workflows.

I believe in continuous learning, adaptability, and delivering organized, reliable work. My goal is to help businesses operate more efficiently by combining administrative expertise, analytical thinking, and technology-driven solutions.
