The dashboard

Default view with both filters set to All.

What I did

  • Built an interactive dashboard in Excel with two dropdown filters: Category and Payment method
  • Used SUMIFS and COUNTIFS formulas, including date criteria, so every tile, table, and chart responds to the filters
  • Added three native Excel charts: share of spending by category, running total through September, and spending by payment method
  • Wrote the summary as formulas, so the written insights update when the filters change
  • Flagged expenses with no receipt so they can be collected before month-end close

Key findings

Largest category: Professional Fees at ₱30,545 (37% of spending). The top three categories make up 70% of the ₱81,663 total.
Most used payment method by value: E-wallet (₱33,591). Spending peaked in the week of Sep 1 to 7 at ₱26,103.
5 of 32 expenses (₱14,277) have no receipt on file. Most missing: Software (2).
Recommendation: collect the missing receipts before month-end close, and review the largest category for savings or a cheaper plan.
