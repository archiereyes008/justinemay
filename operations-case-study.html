Problem

Payroll, vendor payments, and bank records often live in separate sheets, and small errors hide between them. A duplicate employee ID, a wrong pay rate, or an unmatched bank transaction can go unnoticed until it becomes a real cost. This sample project shows how I organize that kind of data so errors are easy to spot and the numbers can be trusted.

What I did

I built a three-part workbook in Google Sheets using a sample dataset.

Payroll

Added formula-driven columns for regular pay, overtime pay (1.5x rate), gross pay, and net pay. Formatted all money fields as currency, froze the header row, and added filters. Built a payroll summary with total gross, total net, total overtime, highest and lowest net pay, and headcount by department.

Vendor payments

Added balance due and payment status (Paid in Full, Underpaid, Unpaid, Overpaid) with formulas. Built a summary of total billed, total paid, total outstanding, total overpaid, and overdue unpaid invoices as of 9/30. Sorted by largest balance first so the biggest follow-ups come up top.

Bank reconciliation

Compared the bank feed against the cash ledger and listed what appears in only one of them, plus the total of matched transactions.

Issues Found

I also logged every payroll anomaly in a separate Issues Found sheet and highlighted those rows, so a reviewer can see what needs checking and why.
