Data Cleaning: Client Contact List

A messy client export turned into a clean, consistent record set, with a log of every issue found and fixed. All names and contact details are fictional sample data.

42Rows in raw export
36Clean records
6Duplicates removed
4Missing emails flagged

What I did

  • Received a messy client contact export with duplicates, mixed formats, and inconsistent spelling
  • Standardized names, emails, phone numbers, cities, statuses, and dates across every record
  • Removed duplicate rows by matching on Client ID and kept the first entry
  • Flagged records with missing emails instead of guessing, so they can be followed up
  • Documented every issue, the rows affected, and the fix applied in a cleaning log
