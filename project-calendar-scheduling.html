Time management

Calendar & Scheduling

A booking system that checks every request against the calendar before anyone is told "yes", so there are no double bookings and no missed meetings.

Sample project for Brightwell Studio (a fictional photography business). All names and events are made up.

One calendar, one truth

Every booking, call, and editing block lives in a single shared calendar.

15-minute buffer

No two client events back to back, so there is travel and prep time.

Protected time

Lunch (12:00 to 1:00) and editing blocks are never offered to clients.

Two reminders

Client gets a reminder 24 hours and 1 hour before every meeting.

The booking workflow

1

Request arrives

By form, email, or message. Copy date, time, length, and client name.

2

Check the calendar

Look for overlaps, buffer gaps, and protected time.

3

Confirm or offer 2 options

Send a reply within 2 hours. Always give two alternatives, never just "no".

4

Add to calendar

Add the event with the client's details, location, and a meeting link.

5

Send reminders

Automatic reminders go out 24 hours and 1 hour before.

Setup behind this: 1 shared calendar with 4 color categories, a booking form with an availability limit, 6 saved confirmation templates, and a weekly Sunday check of the coming 7 days.
